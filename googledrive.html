📌 How to get a working shareable link

  1. Open your video in Google Drive.
  2. Right-click the file → Share → Share again.
  3. Under “General access”, change Restricted to “Anyone with the link” (Viewer access is enough).
  4. Copy the link — it looks like: https://drive.google.com/file/d/FILE_ID/view?usp=sharing
  5. Paste it below and hit Load Video. Add a title and Add to Playlist to save it in your browser.

⚠️ Note: Very large files (>100 MB) or files owned by Workspace accounts with download restrictions may not preview. “Anyone with the link” access is required.
